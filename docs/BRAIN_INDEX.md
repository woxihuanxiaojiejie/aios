## Responsibility Boundary

- BRAIN-001 manages facts and evidence.
- BRAIN-002 manages hypothesis generation and organization.
- BRAIN-003 validates hypotheses through independent analysis and debate.
- BRAIN-004 converts validated hypotheses into decisions and risk-controlled trade plans.
- BRAIN-005 settles outcomes, attributes responsibility and updates system knowledge.

## Core Constraint

The lifecycle order is fixed.

Implementation frameworks may change, but the responsibility boundaries of BRAIN-001 through BRAIN-005 must not be changed without an explicit architecture decision.
